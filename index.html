Smart Energy Management System

Nov 2025 – Jan 2026Solo project

A platform for monitoring the electricity consumption of smart devices. Clients follow their own devices, admins manage users and hardware, and overconsumption alerts arrive in real time.

Design focusMaking live device data readable for a non-technical client, and giving support a clear path from chatbot to a human.
  • Client dashboard: pick a device and a day, then see the hourly consumption as both a line and a bar chart.
  • Separate client and admin experiences, each showing only what that role needs.
  • Support chat that answers common questions automatically (rules or AI) and hands over to an admin when needed.
  • Real-time overconsumption notifications over WebSockets.

TravelPoints

Mar – May 2026Team of 6Agile, 2-week sprints

A platform for discovering tourist attractions: browse and filter places, save favourites, leave reviews and plan trips. I worked across frontend and backend. The parts below are mine.

Design focusTurning raw visit data into a dashboard an admin can read at a glance, and making location search feel instant.
  • Statistics dashboard laid out as summary first, detail after: KPI cards, then top-5 rankings, then hourly and monthly trend charts.
  • "Near me" search. I measured where the delay came from (the browser's geolocation, not the database), then added location preloading and caching so repeat searches are instant.
  • Login, registration and password-reset flows. The reset email now sends a direct link instead of a code to copy.
  • Add-objective form with validation.

Travel Agency Platform

Spring 2025Solo project

A booking platform for a travel agency, used by four kinds of people: clients, employees, managers and admins.

Design focusOne product, four roles: each role gets its own screens and only the actions it needs.
  • Visual catalogue of holiday packages with filtering by destination, price and date, plus sorting.
  • Role-based navigation and dashboards for bookings, clients, users and sales statistics.
  • Interface fully translated into Romanian, English and French, switchable at runtime.
  • Export of packages, users and bookings to CSV, JSON, XML or Word.
